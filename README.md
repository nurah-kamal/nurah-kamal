## What I enjoy working on
 
Problems where the answer isn't obvious straight away. I like taking something messy (a dataset, a process, a system), working out what's actually going on, and then deciding whether it can be improved or automated.
 
The work I'm proudest of is where something manual and hard to analyse became structured and useful: pulling information together from different places, cleaning and validating it, automating parts of the process, and turning it into reporting that people can actually make decisions with. The end result isn't really the dashboard or the script. It's that someone can work differently.

## How I work
 
- **Check the source before trusting the number.** A number that looks right isn't necessarily right. I go back to where the data came from, check what's actually being measured and look for inconsistencies. Often the interesting part isn't the analysis; it's finding out whether the data is telling the truth in the first place.
- **Understand the problem before automating it.** Otherwise you just get the wrong answer faster.
- **When I'm stuck, I stop forcing it.** I break the problem down, check what I actually know, isolate the part that doesn't make sense and test one thing at a time. I'd rather find the cause than guess my way through.
- **Data about people deserves care.** Most of what I work with is about real people, so privacy (POPIA) is part of the design from the start.
- **Presentation matters.** A report that's accurate but hard to read hasn't quite done its job.
A lot of my role also sits between the business and the developers: working out what's needed, reviewing specifications, and making sure a system ends up with the people who'll actually use it.

## The kind of work I do
 
Most of what I build is for my job and covered by confidentiality, so I can't share the code or the details. In general terms, it includes:
 
- **Quality review tooling:** a tool that scores support interactions against a rubric and shows the results on dashboards `React` `Supabase` `LLM API`
- **Tracking and reporting automation:** Sheets and Apps Script workflows that collect data on a schedule, roll it up and feed dashboards, including business-day calculations that account for South African public holidays `Apps Script`
- **Trend dashboards:** showing patterns over time so it's easier to see when something changes and why `Sheets` `Apps Script`
- **Report checking:** automated checks that recalculate figures from the source data before a report goes out `Python`
- **Report pipelines:** turning raw exports into formatted Word and Excel reports `Python` `Node.js`
- **Text-to-speech for dictation practice:** South African English voices, letter-by-letter spelling and adjustable pace `Python` `edge-tts`
I also work alongside developers on larger systems, helping with scoping, reviewing specifications and handover.
